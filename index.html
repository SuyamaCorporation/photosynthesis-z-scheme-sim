What is the Z-Scheme?

The Z-scheme describes the path of electrons during the light-dependent reactions of photosynthesis. Electrons flow from water (high reduction potential) through two photosystems, gaining energy from light, to ultimately reduce NADP⁺ to NADPH.

Electron Flow Steps

  1. 1 Water splitting: 2H₂O → O₂ + 4H⁺ + 4e⁻ at OEC
  2. 2 P680 absorbs 680nm light → excited P680* (−0.8V)
  3. 3 Electron passes to Pheophytin → QA → QB (−0.6V)
  4. 4 Plastoquinone (PQ) carries e⁻ + H⁺ across membrane
  5. 5 Cyt b6f: e⁻ transfer + H⁺ pumping → ATP synthesis
  6. 6 P700 absorbs 700nm → excited P700* (−1.0V)
  7. 7 Ferredoxin (Fd) accepts electrons from PSI
  8. 8 FNR reduces NADP⁺ + H⁺ → NADPH (final product)
